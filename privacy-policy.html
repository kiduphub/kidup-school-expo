KidUp Hub – Privacy Information

Event: KidUp Hub School Expo, 6 November 2026.

We collect the registration details you provide, including parent name, phone, email, number of attendees, child's age range, current nursery/school where provided, and any questions. We use these to manage event registration, contact you about the event, and coordinate attendance.

Registrations are stored in a restricted-access Google Sheet. We may use Meta Pixel only after you opt in to optional advertising tracking; it measures page visits and completed registrations. We do not intentionally send form details such as name, phone, email or children's details to Meta through Pixel events.

We share registration details only with authorized event organizers or service providers where necessary for managing the event, subject to applicable requirements. You may request access, correction, or deletion of your registration information by contacting the organizer.

Before launch: The event organizer must add a real contact email/phone, a data-retention period, legal/business identity, and review this notice against applicable Egyptian law.
