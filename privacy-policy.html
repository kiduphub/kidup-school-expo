KidUp Hub · School Expo

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 October 2026

This policy explains how KidUp Hub event organizers handle information submitted for the KidUp Hub School Expo taking place on Friday, 6 November 2026 at City Play, Zahraa El-Maadi, Cairo.

1. Information we collect

When you register, we collect the parent or guardian's name, mobile number, email address, number of attendees, child's age range, and any nursery or school name or questions you choose to provide. We may also record the time of registration and the source of your visit (such as campaign UTM tags).

2. How we use your information

  • To process and manage event registrations and attendance.
  • To contact you with updates or practical information about the event.
  • To answer your event-related questions and manage registration changes.
  • To understand aggregate registration performance and the sources of event interest.

3. How information is stored and shared

Registration records are stored using Google Sheets and are intended to be accessible only to authorized event administrators. We may share the minimum information necessary with event organizers or service providers helping us operate the event. We do not sell registration information.

4. Optional advertising measurement

If you choose Accept in the separate tracking notice, the site uses Meta Pixel to measure visits and successful registrations and evaluate event advertising. Declining optional tracking does not prevent you from registering. We do not intentionally include your name, phone number, email address or your child's details in the Pixel events we configure. Meta may independently process technical information under its own privacy terms.

5. Retention period

Our planned retention period for identifiable event registration data is 90 days after the event, through 4 February 2027. After that, event administrators will delete or anonymize these records unless retention is required by applicable law or to address a specific outstanding matter. This is a planned administrative process, not an automatic deletion feature of the registration form.

6. Your choices and requests

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your registration information, subject to applicable requirements, by emailing kiduphub@gmail.com. Please do not send additional children's personal information unless necessary to handle your request.

KidUp Hub event organizers
Email: kiduphub@gmail.com
